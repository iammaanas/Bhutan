FOOTAGE SAFETY · EVERY NIGHT

Capture. Copy. Verify.

The transfer routine should be boringly reliable. Keep at least two verified copies before clearing any card.

01CAPTURE

Shoot the day

Sony SD card plus phone clips. Keep footage organised by date and destination as you go.

02TRANSFER

Card → MacBook Neo

Use the Portronics M52 reader for the Sony card, then collect the day’s phone clips.

03PRIMARY COPY

Copy to the SSD

Move the source footage into clearly named folders on the 256 GB external SSD.

04SECOND COPY

Mirror to both HDDs

Copy the footage to the two 1 TB HDDs. Keep the copies separate where practical.

05VERIFY

Open and play files

Check that files exist and play from the SSD and HDD copies; a completed transfer alone isn’t proof.

06CLOSE OUT

Log the day

Review selects, note missing shots and record a short shot log. Don’t erase a card until copies are verified.

!

Card rule Keep original cards untouched until the SSD and HDD copies have been checked. If time is tight, back up first and review footage later.
