THE CAMERA PLAN

Keep it simple.
Keep it safe.

Primary camera: borrowed Sony ZV-E10 with 18–105 mm lens. Secondary camera: Samsung Galaxy S25. Film from a safe, stationary position or as a passenger; never let a shot distract the driver.

DUMP ROUTINE

Footage safety comes first.

  1. During the day, copy cards to the 256 GB external SSD.
  2. At night, make a second copy to the 1 TB HDD.
  3. Verify both copies before any card is cleared.
  4. Group footage by date and destination for the edit.
